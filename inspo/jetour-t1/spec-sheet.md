Sources: jetour.com.pk (official Pakistan product page, fetched 2026-08-29) and the
official Pakistan launch (2026-08-27/28, PakWheels/ProPakistani coverage).

## Cabin & Tech (official)
- 15.6-inch infotainment screen, 9 Sony speakers
- Rectangular digital driver cluster
- Dual-zone automatic climate control
- "Cloud-like" comfort seats (sage-teal or black trims)
- Level 2 ADAS: AEB, FCW; 540° panoramic camera; 6 airbags
- 80% high-strength steel body
- Quiet cabin, spacious passenger area (official marketing)

## Chassis (launch/press data)
- Track F/R ≈ 1690 / 1700 mm; ground clearance 190–200 mm
- Approach / departure 28° / 29°; wading 600 mm
- Tires 235/65 R18 or 235/60 R19; 19" petal alloys

## Design signatures (vision-verified from press imagery in this folder)
- Front: black full-width grille panel, 2 rows of illuminated white dashes,
  square lamps with 4-point cloverleaf DRLs, chunky black bumper + amber reflectors
- Side: angular black arch cladding, rockers + side steps, louvered C-pillar
  quarter glass, petal two-tone alloys, roof rails, panoramic roof, shark fin
- Rear: vertical tailgate, body-color band with spaced silver JETOUR letters,
  4-point red tail lamps, body-color roof spoiler, no external spare
- Interior: floating 15.6" screen + rectangular cluster, piano-key toggles,
  crystal gear knob, rotary dial, sage leather with black bolsters,
  teal ambient light strips, warm dome light
